Ask Archimedes

Custom test, or ask a question

Type your own comparison - directly (e.g. speed_AVG_mph > speed_limit_infer_mph_corrected + 10) or in plain English (e.g. "segments where the inferred limit and HERE disagree by more than 15 mph") - and it's translated and run against the table/filters above. Or just ask a question, like "what is Moove?" or "what does the Evaluator's cost cap mean?" and Claude answers directly - no query is run for a question. Comparisons are checked against a strict, safe grammar before they ever reach BigQuery; a question never touches BigQuery at all.
